## Who I am
- PhD student in Geophysics, Colorado School of Mines (sponsored researcher from INPEX).
- Background spans seismic signal processing and deep learning.

## Core research
- **DAS (Distributed Acoustic Sensing)** — my primary instrument.
- **DASGeo**: converting DAS strain-rate data to particle velocity.
- **Transformer / attention architectures for seismic data** (foundation models,
  transfer learning, positional encoding, physics-informed front ends such as
  trainable NMO operators).
- Near-surface geophysics, seismic refraction, sparse-geophone interpolation.
- Applications: CCS monitoring, marine DAS + OBN/OBC, Ichthys/Masela-type assets.
